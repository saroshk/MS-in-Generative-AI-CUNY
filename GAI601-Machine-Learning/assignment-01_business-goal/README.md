# Assignment 01 – Business Goal

## Task
Acting as the **business stakeholder**, define what the data scientist should achieve for the bank's term-deposit telemarketing campaign, in a short brief (under 500 words) written before any modeling begins.

## Approach
1. **Business context:** banking sector conditions, market dynamics and competitive pressure, the value of long-term deposits, and telemarketing challenges (low conversion, high cost, customer intrusiveness).
2. **Key business goals:** specific, measurable goals aligned with campaign efficiency, ROI, and cost reduction.
3. **Problem definition:** which clients to target in the next campaign, with term-deposit subscription (yes/no) as the outcome, tied directly to the business goals.
4. **Stakeholders:** additional stakeholders (campaign managers, telemarketing agents, clients, compliance), with their own business goals and expectations, such as interpretability.
5. **Success metrics:** KPIs such as conversion rate, lift, cost per acquisition, cost savings, and ROI, each tied to a business goal.

## Dataset
UCI Bank Marketing dataset: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/222/bank+marketing

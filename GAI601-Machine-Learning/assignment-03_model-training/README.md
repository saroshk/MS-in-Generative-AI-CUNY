Assignment 3: Experimentation & Model Training

Introduction
Data Science is called a science because it follows the scientific method. In Machine Learning, Experimentation refers to the systematic process of designing, running, and analyzing different configurations to identify the optimal settings that performs best. Experimentation is learning by doing. Experimentation involves changing parameters, evaluating results (using metrics), and learning from each experiment to iteratively find the optimal model. 

Experimentation & Model Training
In experimentation, the key is to modify only one thing (variable, algorithm) at a time to isolate the impact of each change and understand its effect on model performance. In the assignment you will conduct at least 6 experiments (in real life, data scientists may run from a few dozen to hundreds of experiments for a data science project).

Do the following:

Experiment (train a model) using one of the Decision Tree algorithms, and for every experiment do the following:
Planning - the Hypothesis
Define the objective of the experiment. What are you trying to learn? What is your theory & how will you test it?
Planning - Change one thing 
Decide what you will change to test that hypothesis. Experiments should modify only one parameter or algorithm at a time. If the results improve you are on the right track, otherwise your hypothesis is wrong.
Train a model - the "Experiment"
Train the model i.e. run an experiment. 
Evaluation
Was you hypothesis true or not? What did you learn?
Planning - Recommendation
What do you recommend for the next experiment (based on the evaluation?). What is your new hypothesis?
Run 6 experiments in total - ensuring that you build on top of each experiment (by learning from the previous one)
